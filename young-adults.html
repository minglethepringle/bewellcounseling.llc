A safe place to say the hard things out loud.

Online therapy for young adults 17 and up in Massachusetts, Illinois and North Carolina.

Finding your footing as a young adult can be challenging.

Between school, friendships, family expectations, and figuring out who you are, there can be a lot to navigate. Sometimes you feel overwhelmed, misunderstood, or unsure of yourself. Other times, you may not even know exactly what’s bothering you. Therapy offers a place to sort through what’s going on, feel heard, and figure out how to make things better.

When trying harder isn’t making it better

  • Do you feel overwhelmed trying to balance work, school, relationships, and everyday responsibilities?
  • Are you questioning your choices or wondering if you’re on the right path?
  • Do you feel like everyone else is moving ahead while you’re struggling to figure things out?
  • Are you feeling disconnected from friends or finding it harder to maintain meaningful relationships?
  • Do you put pressure on yourself to succeed, but still feel like you’re falling short?
  • Are you constantly worrying about your future, your finances, your career, or whether you’re where you’re supposed to be?
  • Do you find yourself feeling anxious, lonely, or dissatisfied, even when things seem to be going well?
  • Are you struggling with major life transitions, independence, or figuring out who you are and what you want?

When things start feeling harder than they should

Maybe you notice that you:

  • experience sad feelings often
  • think you aren’t good enough
  • can’t stop thinking about your problems or how you wish things were different
  • have low or no motivation to do things
  • want to stay in bed more of the time
  • feel tired no matter how long you sleep, or have trouble sleeping
  • don’t enjoy things you used to
  • have difficulty concentrating, or “brain fog”
  • feel like you’re letting yourself or others down
  • forget to get things done
  • aren’t as hungry, or your eating habits have changed

What could feel different?

Treatment can lead to real, noticeable shifts, like:

  • Feeling happier and less overwhelmed or worried
  • Gaining control of the negative thoughts in your head
  • Understanding how to manage your emotions
  • Setting healthier boundaries in relationships
  • Listening better, and communicating your needs more clearly
  • Feeling more confident and better about yourself
  • Creating a more manageable workload
  • Spending time on things and people you enjoy
  • Better eating and sleeping

More in control, more intentional

My goal is to help you feel more confident, understand your emotions, and make choices that feel right for you. Together, we’ll work on finding ways to feel better about yourself, navigate relationships, and handle the challenges that come your way.
