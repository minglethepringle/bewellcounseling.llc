Adults · Anxiety, stress & depression

Time marches on, whether we’re mindful of it or not.

Relationship challenges, life changes, work stress, family dynamics — or simply keeping up with the daily routine.

Maybe things used to seem better, but today they don’t feel great. Maybe a lot has changed; maybe nothing has. Maybe you don’t quite know what you feel, or why.

It’s sometimes hard to understand what’s happening, and it can help to talk with someone. Therapy can help you learn what you’re feeling, why you might be feeling it, and how you can make things better.

Does any of this sound like you?

Doing fine, on paper. Stuck, in practice.

  • Do you feel like you’re doing generally well, but at the end of the day you don’t feel satisfied?
  • Do you know something is missing — maybe even what it would take — but keep focusing on what’s in the way?
  • Do you beat yourself up because you haven’t followed through in the past?
  • Do you question whether what you really want is even attainable?
  • Did something outside your control land you here, leaving you angry or resentful?
  • Has something changed, and now you want to re-examine the best way forward?

I can help you make sense of the thoughts in your head and the way you’re feeling. Together, we can map your way forward.

Support through life’s turning points

I provide support at times when you are:

  • feeling poorly about yourself, or that you’re disappointing others
  • transitioning to college or a new job
  • moving, or ending or beginning a relationship
  • losing, or having lost, someone close to you
  • becoming a parent and raising a child
  • parenting neurodivergent children
  • navigating special education services in schools
  • managing a medical diagnosis or chronic illness
  • creating the life you want after the kids grow up
  • caring for aging parents
  • living with stressful family dynamics

Common signs of anxiety and depression

You might be experiencing:

  • low mood or sadness
  • loss of interest or pleasure
  • feeling hopeless
  • weight loss or gain
  • sleeping too much or too little
  • fatigue
  • feeling worthless
  • difficulty concentrating
  • feeling tense or keyed up
  • restlessness
  • increased worry
  • fear something awful might happen

What therapy can help you do

Move forward in the direction you choose

  • Figure out what you want to be different, and make it better
  • Cope when things don’t go as planned
  • Manage your emotions through the ups and downs
  • Recognize your strengths and gain confidence
  • Regain a sense of control and stability
  • Feel less stressed, less anxious, and happier more of the time
  • Reclaim who you want to be
  • Find purpose, and feel inspired again

My approach with adults

I help adults explore the trouble spots in their lives, identify what they want, and build the habits and awareness to get there. My goal is to help you open up the possibilities for greater contentment.
