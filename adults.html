Find happiness. Worry less.

Therapy for adults facing relationship challenges, life changes, work stress, family dynamics, or simply keeping up with the daily routine.

Maybe things used to seem better, but today they don’t feel great. Maybe a lot has changed; maybe nothing has. Maybe you don’t quite know what you feel, or why.

It’s sometimes hard to understand what’s happening, and it can help to talk with someone. Therapy can help you learn what you’re feeling, why you might be feeling it, and how you can make things better.

Doing fine, on paper. Stuck, in practice.

  • Do you feel like you’re doing generally well, but at the end of the day you don’t feel satisfied?
  • Do you know something is missing, maybe even what it would take, but keep focusing on what’s in the way?
  • Do you beat yourself up because you haven’t followed through in the past?
  • Do you question whether what you really want is even attainable?
  • Did something outside your control land you here, leaving you angry or resentful?
  • Has something changed, and now you want to re-examine the best way forward?

I can help you identify what’s getting in the way of you feeling more at peace and experiencing greater satisfaction with your life today.

Support through life’s turning points

I provide support at times when you are:

  • feeling poorly about yourself, or that you’re disappointing others
  • transitioning to college or a new job
  • moving, or ending or beginning a relationship
  • losing, or having lost, someone close to you
  • becoming a parent and raising a child
  • parenting neurodivergent children
  • navigating special education services in schools
  • managing a medical diagnosis or chronic illness
  • creating the life you want after the kids grow up
  • caring for aging parents
  • living with stressful family dynamics

You know you’re feeling low or worried if…

  • every day it’s the same thing, and every day you do the same thing, and wonder why you can’t make changes
  • you constantly think about what got you to this point, and wonder how it came to be
  • you often wonder what things would be like if things in the past never happened, or were different
  • things feel out of your control, and you don’t even know where to start making changes
  • you wonder who you are and what you want, anyway
  • you wonder where the carefree, happier version of yourself went, and how to get it back
  • you remember being a do-er, and wonder why you don’t feel like one now
  • you have everything you asked for, but right now it’s not what you want
  • it’s hard to find space in your mind for much other than worry, fear and frustration
  • you can’t imagine any way for things to be different with all you’re juggling

Together, we can work so that you:

  • Stop worrying about what others think, or expecting the worst
  • Understand that you can’t be the person who protects everyone else from disappointment
  • Recognize all of the things you’re proud of, and believe you are good enough
  • Feel confident that you are worthy, and that people won’t lose interest or stop loving you
  • Say yes when you mean yes and no when you mean no, and feel okay putting your needs first
  • Choose relationships that leave you feeling seen, heard, important and fulfilled
  • Have difficult conversations, even when you worry about someone’s reaction
  • Accept that you can’t be perfect all the time, and stop blaming yourself when things go wrong
  • Better manage your emotions when you’re reminded of painful past experiences
  • Connect with others, even when you’re afraid to let people get too close

I can help you understand what keeps you stuck, break free from unhelpful patterns, and move forward.
