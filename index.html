I seek to join you on your journey and help you recognize your own capacity for change.

I offer a nurturing, safe space where you can explore what is troubling or challenging to you in the present, and tap into your best way to make things better. I’m deeply empathetic and genuinely enthusiastic about this work. I’m an active participant in it, not a silent note-taker.

I work best with people who bring an open mind, some curiosity about what they’d like to change, and a healthy desire for things to be different.

Sound familiar?

Sometimes it’s hard to say what’s wrong, exactly.

It can help to talk with someone when you need to sort through relationships, life changes, work stress, or family dynamics.

  1. Do you feel like you’re doing generally well, but at the end of the day you don’t feel satisfied?
  2. Does it seem like everyone else has something better going on than you?
  3. Do you often feel overwhelmed by everything on your mind, and unable to make sense of it all?
  4. Do you find yourself feeling bad around certain people, in ways you remember feeling as a kid?

What the first steps look like

I typically return calls the same day, and always within 24 hours.

  1. Reach out

    Email nancy@bewellcounseling.llc, or call 781-771-0687 and let me know the best way to reach you.

  2. A free 15-minute call

    We’ll talk about what you’re looking for, I’ll answer your questions, and together we’ll decide whether we’re a good fit.

  3. Your first session

    We get to know each other and set goals. I like to meet weekly for the first four sessions, then decide together what serves you best.
