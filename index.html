I’m not your typical therapist.

Even deciding to seek therapy can be hard, never mind deciding who to work with. All the acronyms, modalities, trainings and certifications can be overwhelming to decipher. I don’t stand on ceremony, and I don’t lean on credentials to seem better equipped than someone else.

My approach is rooted in compassion for all the messy parts of being human. I show up authentically, with a lot of enthusiasm, and I actively guide the process of getting you where you want to go. I’m an active participant, not a silent note-taker.

Sound familiar?

Sometimes it’s hard to say what’s wrong, exactly.

Sometimes you know something doesn’t feel right, but you can’t quite put your finger on why. You may be doing well in many areas of your life, yet still feel unfulfilled, overwhelmed, or stuck in patterns you don’t understand. Therapy can help you make sense of what’s happening and figure out what needs to change.

  1. Do you feel like you’re doing generally well, but at the end of the day you don’t feel satisfied?
  2. Does it seem like everyone else has something better going on than you?
  3. Do you often feel overwhelmed by everything on your mind, and unable to make sense of it all?
  4. Do you find yourself feeling bad around certain people, in ways you remember feeling as a kid?

Understanding yourself and what’s getting in the way.

  • Why do I do this?
  • Where does this feeling come from?
  • Is it from my past?
  • Is it because of who I am?
  • Was I born this way?
  • Do other people feel this way?
  • What are these feelings trying to tell me?
  • Does everyone else have to work this hard in life?
  • Who am I becoming?

    Send a message through the contact form and let me know the best way to reach you. You can also write to nancy@bewellcounseling.llc or call 781-771-0687 and leave a message.

  2. A free 15-minute call
