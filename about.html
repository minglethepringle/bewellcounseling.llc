Authentic, supportive, and right there with you.

I work with people who want to better understand themselves, navigate life’s challenges, and create meaningful change. Whether you’re feeling stuck, facing a transition, working through difficult experiences, or simply wanting life to feel different, therapy can help you gain clarity, reconnect with yourself, and move forward with greater confidence and purpose.

I’m not your typical therapist.

Even deciding to seek therapy can be hard, never mind deciding who to work with. It can be overwhelming to decipher all the acronyms, modalities, trainings, certifications and education behind every possibility.

In my work, I don’t stand on ceremony, and I don’t look down my nose from what I know, what I’ve accomplished, or what makes me seem better equipped than someone else. My approach is rooted in compassion for all the messy parts of being human: conflicting thoughts, self-doubt, feeling alone, uncertainty, and the struggles we don’t always know how to put into words. I want you to feel comfortable showing up as you are, without worrying about being judged.

I show up authentically, with a lot of enthusiasm and compassion, and I actively guide the process of getting you where you want to go. My desire to help others is rooted in my own experience of being challenged outside my comfort zone to grow as a person.

I provide online counseling for young adults and adults in Massachusetts, Illinois and North Carolina. I enjoy working with people who are curious about themselves, open to new perspectives, and ready to explore what might make life feel different.

More than managing symptoms

I don’t see therapy as just a way to manage symptoms. Feeling less anxious or less down matters, but lasting change usually comes from understanding why certain patterns developed in the first place.

Together, we’ll look at where your patterns come from, why they once made sense, and how to change the ones that are getting in the way now.
