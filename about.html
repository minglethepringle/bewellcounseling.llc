About

Authentic, supportive, and right there with you.

As a licensed clinical social worker in Massachusetts, Illinois and North Carolina, I provide online counseling for teens and adults. I offer a nurturing, safe space where you can explore what is troubling or challenging to you in the present, and tap into your best way to make things better.

I am deeply empathetic, compassionate, and enthusiastic about my work. My desire to help others is rooted in my own experience of being challenged outside my comfort zone to grow as a person.

I work best with people who also bring an open mind, a curiosity to explore and identify what they wish to change, and a healthy desire for things to be different in their life.

I am authentic, supportive, and humble, and I’m an active participant in the therapeutic process. I seek to join you on your journey and support you in recognizing your own capacity for change to achieve your goals.

Modes of treatment

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy
Explore and develop an awareness of the relationship between your thoughts, your behaviors, and your emotions.
Solution-Focused Therapy
Focus on your goals and solutions rather than your problems.
EMDR
Heal from physical and emotional distress related to previous disturbing life experiences. Learn about EMDR.
Strengths-Based
Use your own abilities, knowledge, and capacities to bring about positive change.
Positive Psychology
Draw on your inner resources to overcome challenges, recognize areas of growth, and build resilience and a greater sense of well-being.
