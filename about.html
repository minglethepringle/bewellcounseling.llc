Authentic, supportive, and right there with you.

I’m not your typical therapist.

Even deciding to seek therapy is hard, never mind deciding who to work with. It can be overwhelming to decipher all the acronyms, modalities, trainings, certifications and education behind every possibility.

In my work, I don’t stand on ceremony, and I don’t look down my nose from what I know, what I’ve accomplished, or what makes me seem better equipped than someone else. My approach is rooted in compassion for all the messy parts of being human: your conflicting thoughts, your self-criticism, the feeling that things don’t make sense, feeling alone, feeling scared to admit the truth, and all the other ways we are uniquely individual and uniquely fragile at any given moment.

I show up authentically, with a lot of enthusiasm and compassion, and I actively guide the process of getting you where you want to go. My desire to help others is rooted in my own experience of being challenged outside my comfort zone to grow as a person.

I provide online counseling for teens and adults in Massachusetts, Illinois and North Carolina. I work best with people who bring an open mind, curiosity about what they’d like to change, and a healthy desire for things to be different.
