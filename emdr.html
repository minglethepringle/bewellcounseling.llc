Break free using EMDR.

When the past keeps showing up in the present: EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing) for adults and young adults who have experienced trauma.

I can help you process and heal from past negative experiences that may be shaping the way you experience life today.

Are painful memories, anxiety, or past experiences continuing to affect how you feel and respond today? EMDR helps you process the experiences that may be keeping you stuck in patterns of fear, distress, or self-doubt. Rather than just talking about the past, EMDR helps your brain reprocess difficult memories so they lose their emotional intensity and have less control over your present. The result? Feeling less triggered, more in control, and better able to move forward.

Sometimes, understanding why we feel or respond to things the way we do isn’t always obvious. You may find yourself feeling anxious, overwhelmed, stuck in certain patterns, or reacting in ways you don’t fully understand.

EMDR helps you identify and process experiences that may be contributing to these struggles. Unlike traditional talk therapy, EMDR doesn’t require you to repeatedly talk through or explain everything that happened to you. Instead, it uses a structured approach to help your brain reprocess past experiences and develop new perspectives. This can help you feel less reactive, more at ease, and better able to move forward without being held back by the past.

You don’t have to know what hurt you to begin healing

Not all difficult experiences are obvious or easy to identify as trauma. Sometimes, they are significant events such as abuse, loss, accidents, or other frightening experiences. Other times, they are experiences like growing up feeling criticized, rejected, unsupported, or misunderstood. Even experiences that may seem less significant can leave a lasting emotional impact, shaping how you see yourself, relate to others, and respond to situations today. EMDR can help you process these experiences, even when you aren’t sure exactly what is behind the way you feel.

Feelings that seem older than the moment

  • Around certain people, do you find yourself feeling bad in ways you remember from childhood?
  • Does your inner critic take over in ways you don’t understand, even though life is better than it was?
  • Is it hard to pinpoint why you often feel poorly, or why certain things always bother you?
  • Are you frustrated by relationship dynamics that never seem to change?
  • Do you spend a lot of time wishing the past had gone differently?
  • Do you feel weighed down by all the emotional stuff you’ve been through, and can’t shake it?

More than just talking about the past

Unlike therapies where the work happens mainly through conversation, EMDR taps into your existing healing capabilities using bilateral stimulation (moving your eyes back and forth, or gentle physical tapping) to process negative memories.

It works by linking memories of traumatic events with adaptive, helpful resources, lessening present-day distress and trauma symptoms. EMDR is well researched and effective, and is endorsed by the World Health Organization and the American Psychiatric Association, among others (EMDRIA.org).

When past experiences continue to affect you

  • shock, denial or disbelief
  • anger, irritability, mood swings
  • anxiety, fear or panic attacks
  • guilt, shame, self-blame
  • withdrawing from others
  • feeling sad or hopeless
  • intrusive thoughts out of the blue
  • nightmares or night sweats
  • trouble concentrating or remembering
  • confusion
  • feeling disconnected or numb
  • phobias

EMDR can help you

  • Better manage your big and little emotions
  • Reduce unwelcome emotions that pop up at unexpected times
  • Live more fully in the present
  • See yourself more positively
  • Quiet negative, self-deprecating thoughts
  • Get along better with other people
  • Ease tension and body sensations tied to the past
  • Reduce your fears about the future

I believe in your ability to heal. Together, we’ll uncover what’s holding you back and help you move forward.
