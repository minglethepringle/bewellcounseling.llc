Break free using EMDR.

When the past keeps showing up in the present: EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing) for adults and teens who have experienced trauma.

I can help you process and heal from past negative experiences that may be shaping the way you experience life today.

Trauma can be an identifiable event: physical or sexual violence, gun violence, natural disasters, active military combat. It can also be quieter and more drawn out: abuse or neglect, family conflict, harsh or restrictive parenting, failures in school, bullying or humiliation, being excluded, separation, or a death.

All of these can have a lasting impact on how we see ourselves, how we respond to people, and how we manage our emotions. They can also make it hard to find happiness.

Feelings that seem older than the moment

  • Around certain people, do you find yourself feeling bad in ways you remember from childhood?
  • Does your inner critic take over in ways you don’t understand, even though life is better than it was?
  • Is it hard to pinpoint why you often feel poorly, or why certain things always bother you?
  • Are you frustrated by relationship dynamics that never seem to change?
  • Do you spend a lot of time wishing the past had gone differently?
  • Do you feel weighed down by all the emotional stuff you’ve been through, and can’t shake it?

Healing that doesn’t rely only on talking

Unlike therapies where the work happens mainly through conversation, EMDR taps into your existing healing capabilities using bilateral stimulation (moving your eyes back and forth, or gentle physical tapping) to process negative memories.

It works by linking memories of traumatic events with adaptive, helpful resources, lessening present-day distress and trauma symptoms. EMDR is well researched and effective, and is endorsed by the World Health Organization and the American Psychiatric Association, among others (EMDRIA.org).

Symptoms associated with trauma

  • shock, denial or disbelief
  • anger, irritability, mood swings
  • anxiety, fear or panic attacks
  • guilt, shame, self-blame
  • withdrawing from others
  • feeling sad or hopeless
  • intrusive thoughts out of the blue
  • nightmares or night sweats
  • trouble concentrating or remembering
  • confusion
  • feeling disconnected or numb
  • phobias

EMDR can help you

  • Better manage your big and little emotions
  • Reduce unwelcome emotions that pop up at unexpected times
  • Live more fully in the present
  • See yourself more positively
  • Quiet negative, self-deprecating thoughts
  • Get along better with other people
  • Ease tension and body sensations tied to the past
  • Reduce your fears about the future

I believe you have the capacity to heal, using pathways we will navigate together. I welcome the privilege of accompanying you on your journey.
