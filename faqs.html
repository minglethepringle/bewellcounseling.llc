Getting started

How do I get started?

Email nancy@bewellcounseling.llc, use the contact page, or call 781-771-0687 and let me know the best way to reach you. I’ll be in touch as soon as possible to discuss what you’re looking for, answer your questions, and together we’ll decide if there’s a good fit.

How soon can I expect a call back?

I typically return calls the same day, or at the latest within 24 hours of receiving your message.

What age group do you work with?

I work with clients aged 14 and older.

How does the process work?

After you reach out, I’ll follow up at a mutually convenient time to hear what you’re looking for and answer any questions. If we agree it’s a good fit, we’ll discuss how best to move forward.

Sessions

Which states do you serve?

I’m licensed in Massachusetts, Illinois and North Carolina, and I can work with clients who are located in any of those three states at the time of our sessions.

How long are your sessions?

All sessions are 60 minutes. Depending on your treatment we may meet weekly or bi-weekly, and occasionally a longer session may be recommended. That’s something we’ll decide together. I prefer to meet weekly for the first four sessions so we can get to know one another, then determine the best way to continue.

Are sessions online only?

Yes. All sessions take place through telehealth on a HIPAA-compliant secure platform, so you can meet from anywhere in Massachusetts, Illinois and North Carolina. It’s best to join from a space where you can talk freely and confidentially, such as your home, an office, or a parked car.

How does online therapy work?

You’ll receive an email with a link to our telehealth session; just click it at our scheduled time. You’ll need a computer or tablet with a camera and microphone, and an internet connection. Headphones can help keep things private.

Is online therapy as effective as meeting face to face?

According to the American Psychological Association, online therapy is as effective as traditional face-to-face therapy for treating anxiety and depression, and initial research supports good clinical outcomes for EMDR via telehealth. Telehealth may not be suitable for people with severe mental illness, or for those who pose a safety risk to themselves or others.

What happens during therapy sessions?

In our first session I’ll ask getting-to-know-you questions and explore what you hope to achieve. From there we’ll set treatment goals and session frequency, and I’ll answer any questions about the process. Future sessions focus on supporting you toward those goals, in a way that feels comfortable to you. There’s some paperwork to complete before or during our first meeting.

If it turns out we’re not a good fit, I’m happy to refer you to another therapist with the skills or approach you need.

How long will I be in therapy?

We’ll estimate this at the outset. Some people work on specific challenges over a shorter period; others want longer-term support with life’s ongoing stressors. We’ll set frequency and duration based on your goals.

How do I know what my treatment goals are?

We’ll form them together at the start, based on what you hope to achieve. Goals help focus our work and make it efficient and effective. Since life keeps changing, it’s natural for them to shift over time.

Fees & insurance

How much do you charge?

Sessions are $200 per 60-minute session. All services are billed at this hourly rate.

Do you offer a reduced fee?

Yes, a reduced fee is available upon inquiry. These arrangements are typically meant to support clients experiencing a temporary setback due to an unexpected circumstance.

How do I pay for services?

I accept all major credit cards, as well as HSA and FSA cards, through a HIPAA-compliant online payment portal and IVY Pay, and other methods if needed. Payment is due at the time of the session.

Do you accept insurance?

Be Well Counseling Services LLC is not contracted with insurance companies, so I don’t accept payment through your health insurance directly. I work as an out-of-network provider: payment is due at each session, and I can provide a receipt if you’d like to seek reimbursement through your out-of-network benefits.

Why don’t you accept insurance?

I prefer to focus our work on what you’re struggling with and to tailor treatment to you in the way that will yield the best results. Insurance requirements can limit session length, frequency and duration of treatment, and require a diagnosis to justify services. All of this limits my flexibility to provide the most suitable care.

Can you give me a receipt for out-of-network reimbursement?

Yes. I can provide a receipt for services, called a “superbill,” to submit to your insurer, and guidance at the start on how to navigate out-of-network reimbursement. There’s no guarantee your insurance company will reimburse you.

How do I know what treatment will cost overall?

Under the No Surprises Act, you’re entitled to a Good Faith Estimate of expected charges if you don’t have insurance or choose not to use it. I’ll provide one after we’ve discussed what you hope to accomplish, or before our first session on request. Learn more at cms.gov/nosurprises or call 1-800-985-3059.

Policies

What is your cancellation policy?

We all have responsibilities outside of therapy. At the outset, we’ll agree on a schedule that fits your needs; after that, I simply ask that you treat our time together with courtesy and respect. I prefer 24 hours’ notice of any known cancellation, and I’ll always do my best to reschedule at a mutually convenient time. For last-minute cancellations, I’ll try to reschedule within the same week.

As with any professional relationship, repeated absences will lead to a conversation about a schedule that works, or ending services if regular attendance isn’t possible.
