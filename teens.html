A safe place to say the hard things out loud.

In the ten years leading up to the COVID-19 pandemic, symptoms of depression increased 40% among young people (CDC).

More teens than ever are feeling worried and stressed, and losing hope as they struggle with social pressure, academic expectations and sadness.

The pandemic made all of this harder. I provide a safe, impartial place for teens to talk about difficult topics and make sense of their thoughts and feelings, without judgement and at their own pace.

When trying harder isn’t making it better

  • Do things feel worse even though you try to make them better?
  • Are you sad about changes in your friend group, or the loss of a close friend, and feeling increasingly alone?
  • Does it seem like everyone else has something better going on than you?
  • Does school feel like an endless loop of tests, homework, presentations and projects that never lets up?
  • Does it seem like your parents don’t understand what you’re going through, and nothing you do is good enough?
  • Are you constantly worrying about your future, and whether what you’re doing is enough?

Signs of anxiety and depression

Maybe you notice that you (or your teen):

  • experience sad feelings often
  • think you aren’t good enough
  • can’t stop thinking about your problems or how you wish things were different
  • have low or no motivation to do things
  • want to stay in bed more of the time
  • feel tired no matter how long you sleep, or have trouble sleeping
  • don’t enjoy things you used to
  • have difficulty concentrating, or “brain fog”
  • feel like you’re letting yourself or others down
  • forget to get things done
  • aren’t as hungry, or your eating habits have changed

What can change

Treatment can lead to real, noticeable shifts, like:

  • Feeling happier and less overwhelmed or worried
  • Gaining control of the negative thoughts in your head
  • Understanding how to manage your emotions
  • Setting healthier boundaries in relationships
  • Listening better, and communicating your needs more clearly
  • Feeling more confident and better about yourself
  • Creating a more manageable workload
  • Spending time on things and people you enjoy
  • Better eating and sleeping

More in control, more intentional

I help teens feel in better control of their experience, be more intentional with their choices, and build new habits and awareness that increase their satisfaction.

My goal is to open up the possibilities for greater contentment at school, at home, and with friends.
